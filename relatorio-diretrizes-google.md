## Practical requirements applied

- Provide useful, original crochet instruction for people, clear navigation, readable pages, and accurate descriptions.
- Do not promise AdSense approval or claim editorial choices (such as word counts or image counts) are Google requirements.
- Keep reserved ad areas distinct from content and controls; add no ad code or publisher ID.
- Describe only technologies actually installed in privacy and cookie policies. The starter does not load Analytics or AdSense.
- Keep cookie choices functional; optional tracking is not activated by this starter.
- Use descriptive URLs, crawlable links, responsive images, image dimensions, and structured data reflecting visible content. The current site files use `https://www.cozycrochettrail.com` for canonical URLs; verify that this is the final owned domain before launch.
- Avoid fabricated author credentials, experience, pattern testing, reviews, and statistics.
- For structured data, implement BlogPosting and only visible factual information. FAQ content is visible; FAQPage markup is omitted because eligibility and Google display treatment can change.
- Google documentation says compliance does not guarantee crawling, indexing, ranking, or AdSense approval.

## Classification and limitations

**Official policy/documentation:** AdSense program and publisher policies, invalid traffic and ad placement rules, privacy/consent requirements, Search Essentials and spam policies, image guidance, structured data policies, and Core Web Vitals guidance.

**Project quality practices (not Google guarantees):** beginner-focused original instruction, five topic categories, FAQs where useful, and reserved ad locations. Article length and the number of images are editorial choices, not approval criteria.
