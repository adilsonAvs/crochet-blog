# Pre-AdSense checklist

## Content
- [ ] Review every guide for accuracy, usefulness, originality, and sufficient depth.
- [ ] Verify instructions and US crochet terms; confirm no unverified claims of testing or expertise.
- [ ] Confirm every article meets the project's 1,000-word editorial target (not a Google requirement).
- [ ] Complete human editorial review; remove placeholders and unfinished content.

## Navigation and pages
- [ ] Check all menus, categories, breadcrumbs, internal links, search, and 404 page.
- [ ] Add verified contact and author details; replace all placeholders.
- [ ] Confirm About, Contact, Privacy, Cookie, Terms, and author pages are linked and accurate.

## Privacy and consent
- [ ] Inventory all cookies, storage, scripts, forms, and third-party services actually deployed.
- [ ] Update privacy and cookie notices before enabling analytics or ads.
- [ ] Configure consent and preference controls appropriate to applicable jurisdictions.
- [ ] Verify optional technologies do not run before required consent.

## Images, SEO, and structured data
- [ ] Confirm every image loads, is relevant, has meaningful alt text and dimensions, and is optimized.
- [ ] Replace the example.com host in canonical tags, sitemap, feed, and structured data.
- [ ] Validate sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, metadata, and crawlable links.
- [ ] Validate JSON-LD against visible page content and current Google guidance.

## Accessibility, performance, and devices
- [ ] Check keyboard operation, visible focus, heading order, contrast, labels, and skip link.
- [ ] Review mobile and desktop layouts, image loading, CLS, LCP, and interaction responsiveness.

## Ads and traffic
- [ ] Keep real ad code out until official account details and site review are ready.
- [ ] Verify reserved ad spaces are distinct from controls and content.
- [ ] Do not encourage clicks or use paid/automated traffic.

## Final review
- [ ] Click every link and check every image.
- [ ] Recheck official Google policies on the date of application.
- [ ] Make no promise of approval; Google makes the final decision.
